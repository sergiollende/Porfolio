# Portfolio

## Mode

Experience, with a recruiter reading path: identity → current engineering experience → public projects → technical background → contact.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A professional engineering portfolio that earns credibility through work and specific contributions. No portrait, decorative illustration, skill meters, or hero statistics.

OWN-WORLD: Onest, a cool neutral background, navy text, restrained blue, open editorial rows and fine separators. Project screenshots are the only editorial images; the hero alone has a faint pointer-responsive field with linework.

STORY: Identify Sergio's C#/.NET specialty, read his Enterprise experience, explore public work, then contact him.

FORM: `seed: none (pinned)`. The approved brief specifies “fondo claro neutro, texto oscuro, acento azul discreto, tipografía Onest existente” and the order presentation → experience → projects → profile → contact.

## First viewport

FIRST VIEWPORT: At 1440px, a 1120px content width under a compact persistent header. The left half carries Sergio's name on two lines and the C#/.NET role. The right half explains business software, reliability, and integrations, with links to projects and contact. A quiet baseline names Enterprise Mobility and Madrid. Mobile stacks the same information without hiding copy.

## Interaction

Persistent light/dark theme with a stable toggle name, and language switching that preserves the active section. Native anchor navigation and keyboard focus remain visible. A subtle hero highlight responds to fine pointers only. Content does not move, and no content depends on interaction or JavaScript.

## Evidence and constraints

NoMasOlvidos is operational in testing except WhatsApp Business, has no real users, and stays visibly in development. Claudia is a delivered Astro client website. Screenshots must be authentic public pages without private records. Enterprise is text only. No published phone, supplied CV, hiring badge, or unverified source repository.

## Quality bar

Recruiters can identify C#/.NET, Engineer II at Enterprise Mobility, and project status in a short scan. Experience reads as specific contributions. No broken layouts at 375/768/1440px, both languages, both themes. Contrast meets WCAG AA, controls work with keyboard and storage denial, and every approved content section is present.
